# Website artwork and demo provenance

The metal/stone UI textures and bundled Quadrata font are Blizzard Entertainment's
work. They retain their original ownership and are excluded from the project's
MIT license. See [texture/font sources and hashes](../../assets/wow/NOTICE.md)
and [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Attribution is not a grant
of redistribution rights. This is an independent fan project.

`crest.svg` is original project vector artwork: a chat glyph on a fantasy shield.
It is not an official Blizzard or WhatsApp logo.

The three `demo-*.png` screenshots are rendered with the production app frame,
font and stylesheet, using fictional conversations and a sample chat layout from
`site/demos.json`. They are not screenshots of a linked WhatsApp account. Buttons
inside the images are illustrative; a visible phone icon does not imply call
support. Their fictional adult portraits come from the project's existing
[generated portrait sprite](../../docs/assets/NOTICE.md).

`azeroth-evening.jpg` was created on 2026-11-11 with the built-in image generation
tool via the imagegen skill. It is original generated fantasy environment art,
not a World of Warcraft screenshot. The selected output was converted to JPEG
for web delivery without changing the depicted scene. Original project artwork
is supplied under MIT terms to the extent the project can grant rights.

`social-card.png` is a browser-rendered social preview using the site's branded
artwork and a clearly labelled fictional chat demo.

## Fantasy backdrop prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide cinematic background illustration for a fan-made Warcraft-themed desktop-app website. A richly hand-painted high fantasy scene with the chunky shapes, dramatic atmosphere and painterly detail of classic fantasy game loading-screen art. On the RIGHT half: an enormous ancient stone fortress and distant towers on a crag above a misty valley, subtle warm amber windows and a little torchlight, fantastical mountains dissolving into deep blue-green dusk, a tiny indistinct dragon silhouette far away. Foreground bottom right: weathered stone battlement, ornate aged iron, one warm brazier with a few embers. LEFT half and upper left: mostly deep charcoal-green mist and shadow with restrained texture, visually quiet negative space for large readable gold website typography. Colour palette: aged gold, charcoal, desaturated pine green, ember amber; no bright purple or neon. Beautiful premium atmospheric environment art, majestic but calm, wide landscape composition approximately 16:9. No people, no readable text, no logos, no interface, no chat windows, no modern objects, no watermark. This is a fictional fantasy illustration, not a screenshot of a game. Keep the brightest highlights on the right half and dark safe space on the left.
